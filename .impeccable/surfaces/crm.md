# CRM phase-1 surface

## Direction contract
**THESIS:** A learning-first workbench: every destination exists as a navigable, clearly simulated screen. It refuses a fake dashboard of live-looking metrics.

**OWN-WORLD:** Screenshot-led right-hand navy rail, local Persian UI type, softly lit work canvas, blue state language, precise white table surfaces. No third-party branding or real records.

**STORY:** The learner opens a section, understands the eventual shape of the workflow, and traces its named URL through Django view and template before implementing behavior later.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Right sidebar leads with Binux CRM, generic profile/search notes and five grouped menus. The left pane starts with breadcrumb, exact page heading, explicit demo marker, and one sample data surface or empty state.

**FORM:** User-pinned screenshot translated to a standard Operate layout; seed:77eed987ae9858f1. The signature move is opening a group in the rail while the active destination stays visibly anchored across direct reloads; on mobile, the rail slides in from the right without hiding the page when JavaScript is absent.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
